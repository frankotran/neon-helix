HOW TO PLAY

  • 🌀 Drag to spin the tower. The ball bounces on its own.
  • 🕳️ Fall through gaps to drop. Chain gaps without bouncing for combo multipliers.
  • 🎨 Your ball has a color (top-left chip). Land only on YOUR color — anything else = shatter.
  • 💍 Fly through glowing rings to repaint your ball.
  • 🔺 Red sectors are spikes. Never touch them.
  • 🪙 Grab coins for the shop: new balls, trails, themes.
  • 🔥 FEVER: perfect passes fill the gold meter — full meter = 10s of 2× points.
  • ✨ Floating powerups: 🛡 shield (forgives one mistake), 🧲 magnet, ⭐ 2× star.
  • 🌟 Golden gaps shimmer — thread one for 3× points.
  • 📺 Arm a boost from the menu (watch an ad) to start your next run with a shield or magnet.
  • 🌀 Drifting gaps (level 4+) slowly orbit — time your drop, follow the trail.
  • 💀 SUDDEN DEATH: one life, no revive, triple score.
  • ⏳ Bullet-time pickups slow the world for 5s.
  • 📅 DAILY: one shared tower per day. Keep your 🔥 streak alive.
  • 🎯 Complete missions for bonus coins.
  • 📺 Watch an ad to revive once per run, or double your coins.
  • 🦎 Chameleon gaps cycle colors — drop through only when it matches your ball. Mistimed? You bounce, no death.
  • 🔥 Perfect chains: consecutive PERFECTs multiply coin drops (x2 at 3, x3 at 5, x5 at 8).
  • 🎁 Milestone chests every 25 rows — coin burst, 10% chance at a free skin.
  • 👯 DUAL DROP: two balls, 2× score — but one wrong color kills both.
  • 👻 Skip tokens (shop) phase you through the next row untouched.
  • ❄️ Streak freezes (daily reward screen) protect your login streak if you miss a day.
